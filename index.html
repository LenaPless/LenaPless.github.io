About Me

I am a third-year PhD student at the Center for Astrophysics and Supercomputing at Swinburne University of Technology, working on the gas conditions of galaxies at intermediate redshifts using data from the James Webb Space Telescope. I'm particularly interested in finding good analogs for the first low-mass galaxies that kicked off the Epoch of Reionization to better help us understand how the Universe underwent its last major phase transition.
I have constructed an expansive grid of emission line strengths using the photoionization code cloudy based on the BPASS stellar population models dependent on important interstellar medium quantities such as the metallicity, density, and ionization parameter. By fitting observed emission lines to this grid, I can then constrain the gas conditions in galaxies.
